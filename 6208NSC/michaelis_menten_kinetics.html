Mass-action model

ds/dt = −k₁es + k₋₁c
de/dt = −k₁es + (k₋₁+k₂)c
dc/dt = k₁es − (k₋₁+k₂)c
dp/dt = k₂c

Initial state: s=1, e=e₀, c=0 and p=0. The numerical solution should preserve e+c=e₀ and s+c+p=1, apart from small numerical error. When e₀=0, no reaction occurs.

Approximation curves: equilibrium uses ds/dt≈0, giving dp/dt=k₂e₀s/(K1+s) with K1=k₋₁/k₁. QSSA uses dc/dt≈0, giving dp/dt=k₂e₀s/(KM+s).
